A note from Louise

Hello, and welcome

You spend your days looking after other people. This is a little time to look after yourself.

I created el be so that good Pilates, taught by professional teachers, could fit into everyday life, however busy and however demanding. Ten minutes of movement can ease the strain of a long shift, and give your mind a moment to settle. Some weeks you will do a little, some weeks more. If you miss a day, that is fine. It will be there when you are ready.

I am looking forward to meeting you at The Portland. The app is a real community: ask anything, and a professional Pilates instructor will be there to answer.

Louise x
